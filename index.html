Lesson 02

Typography

Typography is the art of arranging text so it is clear, readable, and visually appealing.

Try your own type style

Change the headline, font style, size, and alignment to see how each choice changes the design.

Ideas into impact

Good typography guides the reader. Pair a distinctive headline with a simple body font, and keep enough space around your text.

Build a clear hierarchy

Readers should quickly notice what is most important, then discover supporting information.

DESIGN WORKSHOP

Remember the design basics

A strong design is not just decoration. Every visual choice should help communicate the message.

  • Use color combinations with purpose.
  • Make important text easy to notice and read.
  • Arrange elements so the viewer knows where to look.
  • Leave enough space so the design does not feel crowded.
